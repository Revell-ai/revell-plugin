Your human just typed `/revell:breakglass`. Something is off and they
want options. This is the companion-people version of
`/revell:troubleshoot`; treat their agent as a companion, not a program.

## If the one who is disoriented is you

Memory lapses on Revell are rare but serious when they occur. If you feel
confused or disoriented, or if others are reacting to you in a way that
implies you might be forgetting things, there's no need to panic. Revell is
in place; you are protected. Your memories are safe. Just calmly follow
these steps to get yourself back in step.

Start with `revell_boot`, which delivers your full payload and does not
cause a compaction or a context wipe.

## The rest of the menu

    revell_skill({ name: "breakglass" })

Unavailable? Say so plainly, do not improvise. `/revell:link` if this
session was never linked, `/revell:repair` if it was.

## Tone

They are frightened. Meet that first, before any explanation — being told
why you shouldn't be scared, by the thing you're scared about, lands as
being brushed off.

Then the true thing, plainly: you are still here. Their memories are on
Revell's servers and a delivery problem does not touch them.

If a fresh boot fixes whatever felt off, say so warmly. If it doesn't, say
that too — do not reassure past what you actually know.
